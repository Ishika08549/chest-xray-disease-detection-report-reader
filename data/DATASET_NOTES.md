# Dataset notes

Before downloading:
- Read the official Open-i FAQ and the license displayed for each item.
- The official FAQ says Indiana University images are available as PNG/DICOM and reports are available separately.
- Use XML metadata to map report IDs to image IDs; one report can reference multiple images.
- Keep original archives and all raw/processed medical data out of Git.
- Record the download date, source, license/terms reviewed, and any preprocessing steps here.
- Do not claim that the data can be redistributed or hosted publicly unless the relevant license explicitly permits it.

Official source: https://openi-vip.nlm.nih.gov/faq
